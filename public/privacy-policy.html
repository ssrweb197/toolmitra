Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolMitra handles information when you visit this website and use the AdSense Readiness Checker.

Information you submit

When you run a scan, you submit a website address. The checker uses that address to request publicly accessible pages and website resources, such as the homepage, robots.txt, sitemap and selected internal pages, so it can produce the requested report. Do not submit private URLs, credentials or confidential information.

How scan information is used

The submitted website address and the public responses retrieved from that website are processed to generate the scan results. ToolMitra is not designed to create a user account or to ask for passwords. We do not intentionally use the scan form to collect account credentials. Hosting providers and infrastructure services may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, browser details and request timestamps, as part of operating and securing the website.

Cookies and local storage

The checker does not require an account. This website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies through the checker code. However, hosting, security, embedded or third-party services may use their own technologies under their respective policies. This policy will be updated if ToolMitra adds analytics, advertising or other third-party integrations.

Third-party websites

When you submit a website address, the checker requests pages from that website. Those websites are controlled by their respective owners and may have their own privacy practices. ToolMitra is not responsible for their content or privacy policies. Links to external websites are provided for convenience.

Data retention and security

ToolMitra does not provide a feature for saving scan reports to a personal account. Technical logs may be retained by hosting or infrastructure providers according to their operational, security and retention practices. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

ToolMitra is a general website utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact brandblitzmotivaion@gmail.com.

Note: This policy is a general description of the current checker workflow, not legal advice. Review it against the actual services, hosting configuration and laws applicable to your operation before publishing.

Website tool requests

When you use an available checker, the URL you submit is sent to the relevant ToolMitra service endpoint. The service attempts to retrieve publicly accessible information from that website, such as its homepage HTML and selected standard resources. The response is used to prepare the result shown to you. The checkers are not intended for private, password-protected or confidential websites.

Information you should not submit

Submit only public website addresses that you own or are authorised to check. Do not submit login credentials, private intranet addresses, personal records, payment information or confidential URLs.

Advertising and analytics

If advertising or analytics services are added to ToolMitra, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies. This page should be reviewed and updated before enabling any such service, including Google AdSense or analytics tags. Where applicable, visitors can review the relevant provider's privacy and personalisation controls.

External links and services

ToolMitra may link to third-party services or documentation. Those services operate independently and their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. ToolMitra does not control how an external website handles information.

Your choices

You can choose not to use a checker or not to submit a website address. You may contact us with privacy questions using the email address above. Applicable privacy rights and available request procedures depend on the laws that apply to you.

Operational note: this policy describes the intended current website-check workflow. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical logs. Review this page whenever code, hosting, analytics, advertising or data-handling practices change. This is general information, not legal advice.
