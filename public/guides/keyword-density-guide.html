Keyword Density Checker Guide

Use keyword frequency as a writing diagnostic without over-optimizing or stuffing keywords into your content.

Open Keyword Density Checker →

What this tool does

Keyword density is the share of words or phrases represented by a keyword in a piece of text. It can help identify repetition, but there is no universal ideal percentage that guarantees search performance.

How to use it

Paste your draft into the checker, review repeated terms and phrases, then read the content aloud to see whether wording feels natural.

Practical tips and best practices

Use the language your audience uses, answer the topic comprehensively and add related concepts where they genuinely help. Use headings to organize the subject, not to force exact-match phrases.

Important limitations

Do not rewrite useful sentences merely to hit a density target. Search engines evaluate relevance and usefulness in more complex ways than a single percentage.

Frequently asked questions

What is the ideal keyword density?

There is no universal percentage that guarantees ranking.

Does repeating a keyword improve SEO?

Excessive repetition can make writing unnatural and may be treated as keyword stuffing.

Should I include related terms?

Include them naturally when they clarify the subject.

Can this tool understand search intent?

No. It counts text patterns; the writer must assess intent and usefulness.

Does punctuation affect counts?

Tokenization rules can affect how words and phrases are counted.
