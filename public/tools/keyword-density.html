How to use the keyword density checker

  1. Paste your article or page copy into the text box.
  2. Enter the keyword or phrase you want to review.
  3. Select Analyze text to see its occurrence count and estimated density.
  4. Review the most frequent words and edit your writing naturally.

What does keyword density mean?

Keyword density is the percentage of words in a text represented by a chosen keyword or phrase. It can help you understand how often a topic phrase appears, but it is not a guaranteed ranking factor or a target you should try to maximise. Write for readers first, use relevant language naturally, and avoid repetitive keyword stuffing.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good keyword density?

There is no universal ideal percentage. Use the phrase where it is useful and make sure the page answers the reader’s intent.

Does this tool upload my text?

The analysis runs in your browser on the text you paste into this page. It does not need to send your article to a server.

Can I check a multi-word phrase?

Yes. Enter a phrase in the target keyword field to count its appearances in the pasted text.
