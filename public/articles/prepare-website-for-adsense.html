How to Prepare a Website for Google AdSense Review

A careful website preparation checklist covering useful content, navigation, policies and technical access.

AdSense approval is decided by Google after reviewing a site and its compliance with current program requirements. A checklist can help site owners prepare, but no outside tool or fixed page count can guarantee approval.

Publish original, useful content

Build pages that answer real visitor questions. Explain processes, include accurate examples and keep information current. Avoid thin, copied or automatically generated pages that add little value.

Make the site easy to navigate

Use clear menus, working internal links and a logical page structure. Visitors should be able to find the main content and understand who operates the site.

Provide clear site information

Publish accurate About and Contact information and a privacy policy that reflects the site's actual data practices. Add other disclosures where relevant to the site and its audience.

Check technical accessibility

Confirm the site loads reliably, works on mobile, uses HTTPS and does not unintentionally block crawlers from important pages. Review robots.txt, sitemap and redirects.

Review policies

Read Google's current publisher policies and program requirements. Remove prohibited content and ensure the site's content, navigation and ad placement plans comply.

Apply and monitor status

Submit the correct site in AdSense and follow the status shown in the account. If action is requested, address the specific issue before resubmitting.

Approval is not guaranteed by a certain number of articles, a domain extension, a checklist score or the presence of policy pages. Always check current official Google requirements.
