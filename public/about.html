About ToolMitra

ToolMitra is an independent website utility project designed to make common website checks easier to understand. Our current tool provides a preliminary review of publicly accessible website signals that may be useful when preparing for an AdSense application.

What the checker reviews

The AdSense Readiness Checker reviews a selection of technical SEO, crawlability, website policy-page, content-structure and navigation signals. It provides a score, a checklist and suggested areas to review.

Independent guidance

ToolMitra is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Google or Google AdSense. Automated checks are heuristic indicators only. They cannot assess every page, determine complete policy compliance, predict an account decision or guarantee AdSense approval. Please review Google's current programme policies and your own website carefully.

Our approach

We aim to present practical, understandable website guidance and to be transparent about the limits of automated checks. If you notice an issue or have a suggestion, please contact us.

Our tools

ToolMitra currently provides an AdSense Readiness Checker and a growing set of independent website utilities: SEO Audit Checker, Website Health Checker, Meta Tags Checker, and Sitemap & Robots Checker. These tools are designed to make selected technical website signals easier to review.

Who can use ToolMitra?

Website owners, bloggers, publishers, developers and small-business teams can use the tools to review publicly accessible website information. No account is required for the website checks currently offered.

How to interpret results

Reports are preliminary and automated. They may not detect every issue, and a pass result is not a guarantee of search visibility, website security, AdSense approval or compliance with any third-party programme. Always verify important findings and consult the relevant official documentation.
