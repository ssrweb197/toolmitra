What does this checker review?

ToolMitra checks public technical and website-structure signals that may help identify common gaps. A strong score does not guarantee AdSense approval. Google also evaluates original content, usefulness, site quality, navigation, account eligibility and programme policies.

Technical & SEOHTTPS, security headers, titles, metadata, crawl settings, sitemap and indexability.
Pages & policiesPrivacy, About, Contact, terms and visible cookie / advertising disclosures.
Content & experienceContent-depth signals, headings, images, navigation and sampled internal links.
